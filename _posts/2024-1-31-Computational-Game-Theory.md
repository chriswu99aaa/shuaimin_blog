
## Week1

### Stackleberg Game

**Main Theorem**

* Assumption: Let f(x) be a function on continuous space X and both f'(x) and f''(x) be continuous
* Necessary condition: If x' is a local or global maximum point, then either x' is a point at the boundary of X or f'(x')=0
* Sufficient Condition: If f''(x')< 0, then x' is a local or global maximum point.
